ABOUT THE PROJECT

What is SecureDocs?

SecureDocs is a blockchain-based document management and authentication system that ensures the integrity, authenticity and lifecycle management of digital documents. It provides a secure, transparent and decentralized way to store, verify and manage documents using cryptographic hashing and blockchain technology.
